# We Drive

A top-down, endless-driving game written in C with [raylib](https://www.raylib.com/).
Keep the pedal down along the infinite road, weave around obstacles, and stay
alive until the fuel runs out.

## Features

- Infinite procedural road with drifting, skidmarks, and speed-reactive camera zoom
- Obstacles that damage the car and health kits that repair it
- Fuel system: burn fuel driving, hold `R` to refuel, refueling requires the car to stop burning
- Low-fuel and critical-damage warnings
- Procedurally generated sound effects (engine rev, drift screech, crashes, pickups, alerts)
- Crisp HUD with custom Rajdhani fonts (OFL licensed) and window resizing

## Getting Started

**Requirements**

- MinGW-w64 toolchain ([w64devkit](https://github.com/skeeto/w64devkit) recommended)
- [raylib](https://github.com/raysan5/raylib) — expects headers/libs at `C:/raylib/raylib` (override with `RAYLIB_PATH`, see `Makefile`)

**Build**

```sh
make            # or: mingw32-make
./wedrive
```

The executable is always named `wedrive(.exe)`.

**Development**

- VS Code: open this workspace, press `F5` (builds debug and launches under gdb).
- Clean rebuild: `make clean && make`.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `W` / `UP` | Accelerate |
| `S` / `DOWN` | Brake / reverse |
| `A` / `LEFT`, `D` / `RIGHT` | Steer |
| `R` | Refuel |
| `ENTER` | Restart when wrecked |

## Gameplay Notes

- Hold `R` anywhere on the road to refuel — it works while driving and never exceeds full.
  Fuel drains faster the faster you go.
- Crash into obstacles to lose health; collect green health kits to repair. Spare your
  car — at critical health a warning flashes, and at zero it's a wreck.
- Drifting is your best tool for tight avoidance: steering hard at speed drops the car's
  grip, leaving skidmarks behind you.
